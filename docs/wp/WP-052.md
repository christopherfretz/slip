- Status: in-progress

**Amended by M2** (the reconciliation of `spec/exec.md` §5.2, §8.4 and
§8.6 with `spec/edits.md`): a round's parameters reach the verify task
in the job (`Job.post`, protocol 2), not on the worker's command line.
The passages changed are marked "amended by M2" below; they are scope
item 5, the two paragraphs on `args.rs` and `commands.rs`, and "One
place makes `Params`". `Job.post`, `Post` and `PostImage` are

**Waits for WP-050** (`slip_digest::map::with_input`, `MapTask`, and the
file `worker.rs`). It does not wait for the digest's open areas: a unit of
an unfinished area is indeterminate, the job reports it as an
`indeterminate` finding, and nothing here changes when the area lands.

4. **`slip_cli::locate`, first form**: `Side`, `take`, `wanted`, `fill`

5. **The worker** (amended by M2): `PhaseTask` (by `job.phase`); the
   worker's two options `--findings` and `--locate` (`spec/exec.md`

## Interface

`spec/exec.md` §2.2 (the store), §8.6 (`image.rs`, `worker.rs`,
`verify_job.rs`), `spec/verify.md` §6.1 "Interface" (`locate.rs`). They
are fixed: WP-053, WP-054 and WP-055's commands are written against them.

**One place makes `Params`.** `verify_job::verify(store, job, params)`
takes a job's parameters as an argument, and nothing in `verify_job.rs`,
`image.rs` or `locate.rs` reads the worker's arguments or keeps a
parameter of its own. `worker.rs` has one function that makes the
`Params` of a job (amended by M2): from `job.post`, the job's store,
and the worker's `findings` and `locate`; the `Declared`, its
`RuleTable` and the image are kept from one job to the next while the
ids in `post` are the same (`spec/exec.md` §8.4). M2's overlay image is
then a change to `Image` and to nothing else of yours.

What you build on, as it is today:

- `Store::read_shard` and `publish_shard`
  (`crates/slip_exec/src/store.rs:539`, `:514`); the store validates what
  it publishes, which is how `publish_declared` is to behave
  (`publish_plan`, `store.rs:472`). `Declared` and its container:
  `crates/slip_model/src/declared.rs`, `formats::content_id`
  (`crates/slip_model/src/formats/mod.rs:401`).
- `parse_observer_with` takes the shard's observer id from
  `observer.id` and the command from `observer.command`
  (`crates/slip_kiln/src/parse.rs:141`, `:152`): `Image::parse` on the
  post side passes a copy of the observer with the post command and the
  **same** id.
- `slip_verify`: `comparable` (`crates/slip_verify/src/compare.rs:99`),
  `at_sums` (`:150`, giving `AtSums::Differing(G)`), `compare_groups`
  (`:508`), `verdict`, `verified`, `unverifiable` (`:584`, `:604`,
  `:620`), `Exemptions::from_declared` (`:58`).
- `slip_digest`: `Detail::Differing` for step 1 and `Detail::Groups` for
  step 4 (`crates/slip_digest/src/lib.rs:236`); `Options.names` for the
  names of this package's `locate` (`lib.rs:247`); `map::with_input`
  and `map::counters` (WP-050).

Rules the spec states and a quick reading may miss:

- **A parse changes the current directory** of the process
  (`parse.rs:160`). Every path the task keeps is absolute.
- **Step 1 with an unusable pre shard** still parses the post-image
  (detail `none`): a post-image that does not parse is `not_parsed`
  whatever the pre shard is; only then is P1 to P3 checked.
- **G1** is over the `inputs` of the step 1 shard (real paths), against
  the subject roots as bytes.
- **G2** compares the whole `FileRef` (path, size, digest), entry for
  entry; **R2** compares the `digests` sections with every group's unit
  list removed on both sides.
- **The post shard** has `digests` without unit lists even when step 1
  produced them (§5.4), and its counters are sorted by name with no name
  twice (`spec/model.md` §8.1).
- **The order of findings is not yours**: `compare_groups` gives it and
  `verdict` cuts it to `limit`. WP-055 changes it from the kind's
  number to the rank of `spec/verify.md` §6 while you work; a test here
  that needs findings of several kinds asserts them as a set, or by
  that section's table after merging `origin/main`, and `locate` takes
  "the first `locate`" from the verdict as it comes.
- **A `CompareError` is `Err`**, with the error's text and the observer's
  main file: the job fails; no verdict is invented.
- **Step 6** runs only when the verdict has a finding and `locate` is
  not 0. The pre side is taken inside step 4's parse, after the
  comparison: the closure of step 4 therefore receives what the
  comparison needs from step 1 (the post units of `G`) and returns the
  comparison and the pre `Side`. The post side is a second parse; if its
  `tu` is not step 1's, the verdict is `unverifiable` (G3) and the
  findings are dropped.
- `verify.reparsed` counts parses, also one whose result was a guard's
  failure.

  `crates/slip_kiln/tests/parse.rs:22` does.

  `--locate`; `--declared`, `--root` and `--post` are not options of a
  worker). In `verify_job.rs`: a job without `post`; a `post` whose
  `declared` is not in the store, is not a `DECL`, or has another id; a
  `post` with an overlay image (the `Err` above); `from_post` of
  `to_post` is the image (amended by M2).
- A **wrong-`verified` sweep**, in `verify_job.rs`: for each of the
  small trees of the test, every single-token edit from a fixed list
  (a literal, a callee's name where an overload exists, a type, a
  qualifier, a default argument) is applied to the post tree, and the
  verdict must not be `verified`. This is the property the package
  exists for; the list and its size are in the handback.
- While the walk has pending areas the trees of these tests have
  indeterminate units (every function with a body until WP-034 is on
  main: `crates/slip_digest/src/stmt.rs:11`; the order unit is written
  since WP-033):
  a test that needs `verified` at the sums uses what the walk determines
  at your base, and says in a comment which package lifts the limit. If
  nothing verifies at the sums at your base, say so in the handback; the
  step 3 path is then covered by a hand-made pair of shards through the
  functions step 3 calls, and by WP-053's tests later.
