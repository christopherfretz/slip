## Scope

The four containers a transform's run publishes:

- `spec/edits.md` §6 and §6.1 (`EDTS`), §7.1 (`OVLY`), §8.4 (`Residue`),
  §8.5 (`RSLT`), §10.6 (`RUNT`);
- `spec/model.md` §3 (the tag `slip.basis.v1`) and §5.1 (the four kinds).

Files. New: `crates/slip_model/src/{editset.rs, overlay.rs, result.rs,
transform_run.rs}`, the same four names under `src/formats/`,
`crates/slip_model/tests/editset.rs`. Changed: `src/lib.rs`,
`src/formats/mod.rs`, `src/hash.rs` (the tag).

Starts when WP-060's types are on `main`. Needs nothing of M1 or Kiln.


Kinds `EDTS`, `OVLY`, `RSLT`, `RUNT`, version 1, through `format_impl!`.
`RUNT` is the one that is not deterministic; it is marked as the run
record is (`formats/run.rs`).

Validation: §6.1's table in its order (the `basis` row is not checked by
the format); the overlay's two invariants; for `RSLT`: `agreement` sorted
by `(rule, observer)` with no two equal, `unused` strictly ascending,
`residue` sorted by `(file, start)`, `exempt` strictly ascending, and the
state's consistency that the record alone shows (`overlay` and `declared`
absent iff `state` is `refused`; `refused` has an all-zero tally except
`selected` and `unparsed`). The remaining rows of §8.5's state are the
trusted base's (`docs/wp/WP-070.md`), not the format's.

## Tests

`crates/slip_model/tests/editset.rs`: binary and JSON round trips of the
four kinds; every invariant broken alone, with offset and path; `overlap`
over the cases of §5.4 (shared byte; two empty ranges at one offset with
equal and with different `(rule, slot)`; an empty range at the start, the
end and strictly inside a non-empty one); `basis_id` changes with the
order, with an absent shard and with a shard's id; `Overlay::find`.
