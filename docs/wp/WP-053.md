## Interface

`spec/exec.md` §8.6: `args.rs` (`ImageArgs` and `WorkerArgs` are
WP-052's and do not change), `declare::main`, `units::main`,
`verify_cmd::main`. `commands::dispatch` calls the three.

What you build on, as it is today:

- `commands.rs`: `parse` (`crates/slip_cli/src/commands.rs:161`) is the
  model for `verify`: the exclusive store, the signal handling, the
  selection, `pool_options` (`:208`), `worker_command` (`:354`), the
  summary lines. Share the code; do not copy it. `verify` passes
  `Phase::Verify` and `--round` to `pool::run` and appends its options to
  the worker command (§8.7).
- `pool::Summary` (`spec/exec.md` §5.7): `run` (the record), `run_path`,
  `outcomes`. "This run's shard" is decided from `Summary.run.jobs`
  alone: the `shard` of the observer's last entry against the content id
  of what `Store::read_shard` returns.
- `Store::latest_run(plan, Phase::Map)`
  (`crates/slip_exec/src/store.rs:588`) for the refusal of §8.7.
- `slip_verify::run`: `Header`, `Report::{new, add, verdict, to_json,
  to_text, verdict_line}`, `state` (WP-055). The command reads a map
  shard only for an observer whose verify shard is `diagnosed` or
  `rejected`.
- `Image::from_args`, `Image::parse`, `Side` (WP-052);
  `slip_digest::records::records`, `declare::{read, needs, resolve,
  finish}` (WP-055).

Rules a quick reading may miss:

- **A shard that is not this run's is no shard** for the report
  (`Report::add` with `None`): the observer is `failed`.
- **The `--report` file** is written through a temporary file in the
  same directory and renamed; an interrupted run writes none.
- **`declare` parses in this process**, one observer after another, and a
  parse changes the current directory: `--from`, `--out` and the store
  are made absolute first. `declare` and `units` need no pool and take
  no token beyond the implicit one.
- **`declare` writes nothing on an error**, and its warnings (an image
  that does not parse) do not change its status.
- **Status 4** only for `verify`'s incomplete run; an empty selection is
  one.

## Tests

- `crates/slip_cli/tests/args.rs`: `spec/exec.md` "Tests".
- `crates/slip_cli/tests/verify.rs`: `spec/verify.md` "Tests" and
  `spec/exec.md` "Tests", on trees and databases the test writes, through
  the executable (as `tests/cli.rs` does): each row of the state table
  that a real run can reach (`failed` by a worker killed with a tiny
  `--observer-memory`, or by a stale shard), each run verdict and exit
  status, each input error with its exact line, the text and the JSON
  compared with goldens after the ids, paths and times are masked,
  `declare` for each error of `spec/verify.md` §2.3, `units` with each
  option.
- A relocation through the whole path: `slip parse`, an edited copy of
  the tree, `slip declare`, `slip verify --root --post --declared`. While
  the walk leaves units indeterminate the expected verdict is the one
  your base gives (`not verified` with `mismatch indeterminate_only`
  equal to the number of observers); the test asserts that no finding
  group is of a kind other than `indeterminate` and
  `derived_indeterminate`, and says in a comment which package turns it
  into `verified`. The same tree with one callee changed must show a
  group that is not indeterminate once statements and expressions are on
  main; if they are not at your base, the test is written and marked
  `#[ignore = "needs WP-034..037"]`, and the handback says so.

## Done when

- The full gate passes with `origin/main` merged in.
- **On real code**: with a release build and the configured `llvm`
  corpus, `slip parse --db <database> --only /llvm/lib/Support/` into a
  store under the corpus directory, then `slip verify --plan <id>` (the
  identity, nothing declared) with `--report`. In the handback: the two
  outputs in full, `wall` of each, and the sum of `verify.reparsed`.
  Every observer is expected `mismatch` and `indeterminate_only` until
  the walk is complete; an observer in another state, or a finding group
  of another kind, is a finding to hand back (it is invariant (b) failing
  for a reason that is not the walk's unfinished areas), not something to
  fix here.
- `slip --help` shows the three commands; `spec/exec.md` §8 is the text.
