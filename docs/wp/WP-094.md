# WP-094 — Demo 3: a C++ transform across LLVM

- Status: todo
- Milestone: M3
- Track: G
- Needs: WP-091, WP-092, WP-093, WP-096
- Size: M

## Scope

Launch after Demo 2 has run and reduce, apply and verify accept what a
programmable transform hands over (`spec/api.md` §7.6 as reconciled with
`spec/edits.md`).

[docs/demos/demo-3.md](../demos/demo-3.md), all of it: run it, check it,
measure it, write it up. This package writes no feature. What it finds
broken goes back as a defect against the package that owns it, with the
smallest subject that shows it; it does not patch around a defect to get
a number.

1. A script `scripts/demo-3.sh` (or an `xtask` command, as Demo 2 chose)
   that runs parts A, B and §3.1 from a planned corpus to the report, and
   is re-runnable.
2. The checks of `demo-3.md` §5, each automated: the 36 edits and 12
   groups as a golden diff; the declared units; the twelve spelled
   callees (WP-096); the native-rename differential; the sabotage and
   negative runs; determinism across two worker counts.
3. The independent check of Part A: write the overlay to a copy of the
   tree, build `SupportTests`, run `KnownBitsTest.*`. Needs the
   operator's agreement to the build (ask through the supervisor before
   starting it); everything goes through the jobserver.
4. The measurements of `demo-3.md` §6 in
   `docs/demos/demo-3-record.md`: numbers, machine, commit of Slip and of
   Kiln, and the baseline for the deterministic counters.
5. The report text: what is claimed and what is not, in the words of
   `demo-3.md` §7, with this run's numbers in place of the expectations.

## Interface

None.

## Tests

`crates/slip_cli/tests/demo3_small.rs`: the whole of part A and part B
on a corpus of a dozen files written for the test (a header with two
pairs of functions, callers in three source files, one caller in a
macro argument, one made by a macro body, a same-named member of another
class), from plan to verified overlay, in the gate. The LLVM run is not
in the gate.

## Done when

Every row of `demo-3.md` §5 passes or is recorded as failing with a
defect filed; the record and the report are committed; the supervisor
has the four numbers that decide whether M3 is closed: observers whose
run failed, units exempt, spelled pieces that hold out of those
recorded, and map time with the transform against map time without.

## Out of scope

A second configuration of the corpus (`demo-3.md` §3.1): an operator
decision.
