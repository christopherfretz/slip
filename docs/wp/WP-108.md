# WP-108 — slip_verify: an entity declared in one image and derived in the other is changed (§4 step 4)

- Status: todo

## Scope

The independent review of the pitfall rows R and E
(`docs/wp/WP-058.md`, "Supervisor's handback") found a wrong `verified`
in `spec/verify.md` §4 as it stood: an explicit specialisation removed,
with a `removed` line declared for it, verifies although every caller
now runs the primary template's body. `spec/verify.md` §2.2 and §4 are
amended with this package (step 4 and "Why step 4"); this package makes
`crates/slip_verify/src/compare.rs` do what they say.

1. **Step 4** in `compare` and in the comparison through groups
   (§4.1): where step 2 would give `removed` for a key with a unit in
   the post-image's derived multiset, or `added` for a key with a unit
   in the pre-image's, the finding is `changed`, prevented by `changed`
   only; `removed` and `created` are not used by it; step 3 does not
   count the key. Read §4.1 and say in the landed file why the group
   shortcut cannot skip such a key (step 2 has a finding for it, so its
   declared group differs), and what the derived groups must be opened
   for.
2. **The finding's place and name** (§6, §6.1 "Other findings"): the
   name from the unit record of the side that declares it; `where`
   as the new row of that table says. Row E11's `bad-3` writes its
   place in the subject (`spec/pitfalls.md` §1). Under step 4 the row's
   `bad` tree also gets `changed traits<Mine>` beside the
   `derived_changed` its line names: say so in a comment of its
   `expect.txt`, and if the tree no longer shows what a check of
   declared units alone would miss, add a tree that does (the explicit
   specialisation kept in both images, the difference only in what an
   instantiation finds).
3. **Is the amended §4 now complete?** The soundness argument ("a
   derived unit on one side only is not a difference") is used for keys
   the other image lacks entirely. Try to break it again, and write
   down every attempt with its outcome:
   - a member of a class template explicitly specialised
     (`template <> void S<int>::f() {}`) removed or added;
   - a variable template's, an alias through a class template's member;
   - a partial specialisation removed (the key of `S<int *>` is derived
     on both sides, from another pattern: is that a `derived_changed`,
     and does the instantiation's stream say which pattern made it?);
   - an explicit instantiation definition or declaration added or
     removed (`template class S<int>;`, `extern template`): is the unit
     declared or derived on each side, and is `changed` the right
     finding or a false one?
   - a defaulted special member written (`S() = default;`) where it was
     implicit, and the reverse: declared on one side, and on the other?
   - a deduction guide written where an implicit one was used;
   - a function declared in one image and a builtin or an implicit
     declaration of the same key in the other.
   A case where the finding is wrong either way is reported with its
   pair, not decided here.

## Tests

- `tests/compare.rs`: step 4 in both directions (declared before and
  derived after; derived before and declared after), with and without
  each of the three exemptions (only `changed` prevents; `removed` and
  `created` are then reported unused for the observer), with the
  derived unit indeterminate, with a multiplicity, with the key also
  under a collision; the counters `only_pre` and `only_post` unchanged
  by such a key.
- `tests/groups.rs`: the same through groups, with the declared unit in
  a header group and the derived unit in the translation unit's.
- A pair test of `slip_digest` (`tests/differ/` or the runner's
  fixtures, whichever the suite uses for a finding of the comparison)
  with the two programs of "Why step 4", so that the keys are shown
  equal by the walk and not assumed.
- Mutations: step 4 removed (the `e11` tree and a compare test fail);
  made exemptable by `removed`; made to count in `only_post`; applied
  when the other side has no derived unit (every `removed` becomes
  `changed`: an existing test fails).
