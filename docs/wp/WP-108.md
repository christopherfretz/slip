# WP-108 — slip_verify: an entity declared in one image and derived in the other is changed (§4 step 4)

- Status: done
- Milestone: M1
- Track: C
- Needs: WP-055, WP-058
- Size: S
- Parallel with: WP-070 (which does not touch `compare.rs`), the digest's open packages

## Scope

The independent review of the pitfall rows R and E
(`docs/wp/WP-058.md`, "Supervisor's handback") found a wrong `verified`
in `spec/verify.md` §4 as it stood: an explicit specialisation removed,
with a `removed` line declared for it, verifies although every caller
now runs the primary template's body. `spec/verify.md` §2.2 and §4 are
amended with this package (step 4 and "Why step 4"); this package makes
`crates/slip_verify/src/compare.rs` do what they say.

1. **Step 4** in `compare` and in the comparison through groups
   (§4.1): where step 2 would give `removed` for a key with a unit in
   the post-image's derived multiset, or `added` for a key with a unit
   in the pre-image's, the finding is `changed`, prevented by `changed`
   only; `removed` and `created` are not used by it; step 3 does not
   count the key. Read §4.1 and say in the landed file why the group
   shortcut cannot skip such a key (step 2 has a finding for it, so its
   declared group differs), and what the derived groups must be opened
   for.
2. **The finding's place and name** (§6, §6.1 "Other findings"): the
   name from the unit record of the side that declares it; `where`
   as the new row of that table says. Row E11's `bad-3` writes its
   place in the subject (`spec/pitfalls.md` §1). Under step 4 the row's
   `bad` tree also gets `changed traits<Mine>` beside the
   `derived_changed` its line names: say so in a comment of its
   `expect.txt`, and if the tree no longer shows what a check of
   declared units alone would miss, add a tree that does (the explicit
   specialisation kept in both images, the difference only in what an
   instantiation finds).
3. **Is the amended §4 now complete?** The soundness argument ("a
   derived unit on one side only is not a difference") is used for keys
   the other image lacks entirely. Try to break it again, and write
   down every attempt with its outcome:
   - a member of a class template explicitly specialised
     (`template <> void S<int>::f() {}`) removed or added;
   - a variable template's, an alias through a class template's member;
   - a partial specialisation removed (the key of `S<int *>` is derived
     on both sides, from another pattern: is that a `derived_changed`,
     and does the instantiation's stream say which pattern made it?);
   - an explicit instantiation definition or declaration added or
     removed (`template class S<int>;`, `extern template`): is the unit
     declared or derived on each side, and is `changed` the right
     finding or a false one?
   - a defaulted special member written (`S() = default;`) where it was
     implicit, and the reverse: declared on one side, and on the other?
   - a deduction guide written where an implicit one was used;
   - a function declared in one image and a builtin or an implicit
     declaration of the same key in the other.
   A case where the finding is wrong either way is reported with its
   pair, not decided here.

## Handback

Implementer's handback. The detail is in
`spec/landed/verify/WP-108.md`.

- **Code.** `compare_groups` gathers the derived runs by key first. Then,
  for a key of `D` whose step-2 kind is `removed` (or `added`), it checks
  `Vq(k) − Vp(k)` (or `Vp(k) − Vq(k)`). When that is not empty, the kind
  becomes `changed`, which only the `changed` list can exempt, and step 3
  does not count the key. A key that step 1 makes indeterminate is left
  to step 1.
- **Spec amended (`spec/verify.md` §4 step 4, §4.1).** Step 4 is read on
  the differences of `V`, not on presence. Read on presence, the
  comparison through `G` is not exact: the groups property test fails
  under that reading (mutation M5). On what the walk produces, the two
  readings agree, because of collisions (digest §3.4). Step 4 is now also
  listed in §4.1, with what the derived groups are opened for. The
  finding's `pre`, `post` and `file` are those of a finding about `D`.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/compare.rs`: six tests named `s4_4_*`. `s3_tu_group…` now
    expects `changed`. The reference implementation of
    `s6_order_by_rank_is_a_permutation` implements step 4 and its counts.
  - `tests/groups.rs`: `s4_4_through_groups`, and step-4 coverage in the
    property test.
  - The pair `slip_digest/tests/differ/_/explicit-specialisation-removed`.
  - Row E11: a second `bad` line; `pre-5`/`good-5` with `changed`
    declared; `pre-6`/`good-6`/`bad-6`.
- **Mutations.** The four of the package, twelve of mine and the
  review's five: all 21 are killed under `--no-fail-fast`. The table,
  with each edit and every test that kills it, is in the landed file
  ("Mutations"). `s4_4_step_1_comes_first` was added so that R1 (step 4
  and its exemption before step 1) has a direct test. The row E11 has no
  runner yet (`crates/slip_cli/tests/pitfalls.rs` does not exist, nor
  does `slip verify`), so M1 on the row was shown with an uncommitted
  in-process comparison only.
- **Item 3.** I found no wrong `verified`. I found one class of finding
  on a correct edit that nothing can declare: a partial specialisation
  whose members equal the primary's, removed or added (attempts 3b and
  3c). The instantiation's members differ (`derived_changed`), and no
  exemption reaches a derived key. Step 4 is not involved. Ruling: the
  finding is kept, and the case is in `spec/verify.md` "Open questions".
- **Not run.**
  - The removal of an explicit instantiation, and the reverse of the
    defaulted-member attempts. Both are the added cases with the sides
    exchanged, and step 4 treats the two directions alike.
  - An attempt with ownership rules (§2.1, WP-110).
  - The `where` of a step-4 finding: localisation is not built. Ruling:
    `file` stays the group (`pick.h`); the place is the first declaration
    the source wrote (`pick_int.h:1`), now stated in `spec/verify.md`
    §6.1 "place". Localisation must read it from the chain.
- **Fix round (review).** Mutation table in the landed file;
  `s4_4_step_1_comes_first`; §6.1 "place"; E11 in `spec/pitfalls.md`
  names `good-5` and the `-6` trees; `xfail` for `good`, `good-5` and
  `good-6` (pending areas, WP-045 and WP-041); §4 step 4 names the two
  runs `collides` does not mark; the 3b/3c case in "Open questions".
