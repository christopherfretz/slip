## Scope

`crates/slip_digest/src/expr.rs` and new files `expr_*.rs` beside it.
Tables: `spec/digest-kinds.md` §7 (the rules at its head; `ExprKind`;
the common rows of `ExprNode` and the rows of the kinds listed below),
§7.1 (`ValueCategory`, `ObjectKind`, `ExprFlags`, `ExprDeps`), of §7.2
`UnaryOp`, `BinaryOp`, `CastKind`, `CharKind`, `StringKind`,
`NonOdrUse`, `Radix`, `StorageDuration`, of §7.3 `DeclRefExtra`,
`CompoundAssignExtra`, and §7.4 (`ColdExprData`, `ExprArena`), with
digest §6 and §4.3.

Two things, the first more important than the second:

**The framework**, on which three later packages build in parallel:

- `expr::child` writes one expression as §7 says: the common rows, the
  rows of its kind, the rows of `ColdExprData`, then
  `templ::expr_maps(w, s, e)` (pending, another package's).
- The rules at the head of §7: conditional rows, parentheses (skipped,
  with the one `DependentDecltype` exception, which `profile` needs:
  leave a parameter for it), shared nodes, opaque values (numbered per
  unit: the state lives in `walk::UnitState`, which you may extend),
  overloaded member access, literals from builtin macros (by value).
- One dispatch over `ExprKind` listing all 97 variants. Every kind not
  in this package's list calls a function of its own, `pub(crate) fn
  <snake_kind>(w, s, e)`, body `s.pending(Area::Expr)`, in one of three
  new files that the later packages fill without touching `expr.rs`:
  `expr_init.rs` (construction, initialisation, `new` and `delete`:
  `InitListExpr`, `ImplicitValueInitExpr`, `DesignatedInitExpr`,
  `CXXConstructExpr`, `CXXTemporaryObjectExpr`,
  `CXXInheritedCtorInitExpr`, `CXXDefaultArgExpr`, `CXXDefaultInitExpr`,
  `CXXScalarValueInitExpr`, `CXXParenListInitExpr`, `ArrayInitLoopExpr`,
  `ArrayInitIndexExpr`, `CXXStdInitializerListExpr`,
  `CompoundLiteralExpr`, `NoInitExpr`, `DesignatedInitUpdateExpr`,
  `CXXNewExpr`, `CXXDeleteExpr`, `CXXPseudoDestructorExpr`,
  `CXXThrowExpr`, `CXXRewrittenBinaryOperator`, `ParenListExpr`);
  `expr_dep.rs` (names and constructs of templates:
  `UnresolvedLookupExpr`, `DependentScopeDeclRefExpr`,
  `CXXDependentScopeMemberExpr`, `SubstNonTypeTemplateParmExpr`,
  `SubstNonTypeTemplateParmPackExpr`, `FunctionParmPackExpr`,
  `SizeOfPackExpr`, `RequiresExpr`, `ConceptSpecializationExpr`,
  `LambdaExpr`, `PackExpansionExpr`, `CXXFoldExpr`, `PackIndexingExpr`,
  `CXXUnresolvedConstructExpr`, `CXXExpansionSelectExpr`,
  `IntegerPackExpr`, and `expr::profile`, `expr::profile_expr`, which
  move there as pending functions); `expr_misc.rs` (every other kind:
  `PredefinedExpr`, `SourceLocExpr`, `CXXNoexceptExpr`, `CXXTypeidExpr`,
  the traits, `OffsetOfExpr`, the coroutine kinds, `StmtExpr`,
  `AddrLabelExpr`, `ChooseExpr`, `VAArgExpr`, `AtomicExpr`, the vector
  kinds, `PseudoObjectExpr`, `GenericSelectionExpr`, and the
  `unsupported` kinds `CXXReflectExpr`, `CXXSpliceExpr`,
  `RecoveryExpr`, which you write: indeterminate with their cause, and
  their `indeterminate` tests). The common rows and the cold-map rows
  are written by `expr::child` around the call, so a kind's function
  writes only the rows of its kind. Give the later packages what they
  need to read a node (the accessors you use yourself) and say in
  `lib.rs` how a kind is filled.
- **What bounds the stack.** `walk::DEPTH_LIMIT` (200) is far too low
  for expressions: LLVM has call chains (`StringSwitch<…>().Case(…)…`)
  and operator chains (`a << b << …`, generated `||` chains) thousands
  of levels deep, and an indeterminate unit on a corpus that parses
  fails M1. Kiln builds these on a thread with a 1 GiB stack and a
  stack guard (`slip_kiln::snippet`, `kiln_sema::stack_guard`). Give
  statements and expressions their own nesting count
  (`Walk::enter_node`/`leave_node`, which the statements package will
  use too) with a limit and a documented stack requirement of
  `slip_digest::digest` derived from measured frame sizes (a test
  measures or bounds them); past the limit the unit is
  `Unaccounted("walk/depth")`, never a stack overflow. The tests'
  runner (`tests/common/mod.rs`) then runs each walk on a thread of
  that size. A chain of 20 000 nested member calls and one of 20 000
  nested binary operators must digest without being indeterminate, in
  time linear in the length (tests). If you can make the left spine of
  a chain use no Rust stack at all, do; say which you chose.

`CallExpr`, `CXXMemberCallExpr`, `CXXOperatorCallExpr`,

## Interface

`expr::child`, `child_opt`, `requires_clause` keep their signatures;
`profile` and `profile_expr` keep theirs and move to `expr_dep.rs`
(update the callers' paths in `types.rs`, `keys.rs`, `templ.rs`: that
edit, and `walk.rs` for the unit state and the nesting count, are the
changes outside your files this package may make; `templ.rs` belongs
to WP-029, so change only the path there).

Calls into other areas, all existing on `main`: `value::value`
(`ConstantExpr`'s value, literals where the row says `value`),
`value::string`, `templ::expr_maps`, `local::ordinal` through
`Walk::write_ref` (a reference to a parameter or local variable),
`local::write_decl` (the encoding `decl` in an expression row),
`stmt::child`, `Walk::write_type`, `write_args`, `write_nns`.

Digest §6 is the list of what must and must not be written: the
selected entity wherever one was selected; implicit nodes are nodes;
the "found by argument-dependent lookup" and "had several candidates"
bits are not written when the call is resolved; a qualified member
call's suppression of virtual dispatch is; of a resolved name the
qualifier, the found declaration and the written template arguments
are not (the arguments are when the entity named is a template).
Each of these gets a pair test under `_/` named for the rule, besides
the row tests: they are the reason the tool exists (a root-namespace
migration requalifies names and must change no digest, while a call
that now resolves to another overload, or a call that no longer finds
its callee by argument-dependent lookup but another one, must).

## Tests

Per row, as the common rules say. Expressions are reachable today
through the reach stubs: the initialiser of a namespace-scope variable
(`int x = …;`, `auto x = …;`), a default member initialiser, an
enumerator's initialiser, a `static_assert`, a default argument, and a
function body (`stmt::child` is pending: a test that needs a statement
is marked `# pending`, `# needs: stmt`). Prefer variable initialisers.

Every `CastKind` (47), `BinaryOp` (33) and `UnaryOp` (14) row has its
test: for each, a source that makes Kiln build it (read
`kiln_sema` for the cast kinds that need unusual sources: member
pointer conversions, vector and fixed-point casts, atomics; one Kiln
cannot build from C++ is reported and stays in `untested.txt`).
Must-equal tests: redundant parentheses; `a.f()` against `(a).f()`;
`x` against `::x` against `ns::x` for the same entity; a name found
through a using-declaration against the qualified name; `this->m`
against `m`. Must-differ: the overload chosen; an implicit conversion
added; a user-defined conversion against a converting constructor;
`f(x)` resolving through argument-dependent lookup to another
namespace's `f`; `p->Base::f()` against `p->f()` for a virtual `f`.
The source test WP-024 left for this package: a key does not depend on
the unit that asks (two units naming one entity whose key holds an
expression, e.g. `template<int N> void f(char(*)[N + 1]);`, give one
key) — if it needs `profile`, write it marked `# pending`,
`# needs: expr`, for the package that fills `expr_dep.rs`.

## Rules common to the digest's area packages

Read first, in this order: `docs/AGENTS.md`; `spec/digest.md` (whole);
`spec/digest-kinds.md` §1 and §2, then the sections in Scope; the
documentation at the top of `crates/slip_digest/src/lib.rs` (the
functions every area calls, "How to fill an area");
`spec/landed/digest/WP-024.md`; the Handback of `docs/wp/WP-024.md`.
Look at how `types.rs` and `decl.rs` write their tables before writing
yours.

1. **Files.** You own the files named in Scope and your tests. In
   `crates/slip_digest/src` touch nothing else, with two exceptions: a
   function another area must provide and that has no signature yet is
   added to that area's file as a pending function (final signature,
   body `s.pending(Area::X)`, one line in the handback), never
   implemented; and `lib.rs` gets one line per function you add for
   others, in its table. Do not touch the `Area` enumeration,
   `tables.rs` (generated), `tests/tables/pins.txt`, or the tables of
   `spec/digest-kinds.md`. Several packages run at the same time in
   other files of this crate: a change outside your files conflicts
   with theirs.
2. **The tables decide.** Rows are written in table order as §2.1 says;
   tags and decisions come from `crate::tables`, never from a number
   written by hand; `bit` rows go through `FlagWord`. A set bit or a
   value no row accounts for is `Cause::Unaccounted("<short name>/<row>")`,
   an `unsupported` row `Cause::Unsupported("<short name>/<row>")`; the
   stream stays well formed either way. A row that is wrong about Kiln
   (the field is not stored as the note says, a fact is missing) is not
   corrected in code: write the conservative thing (indeterminate),
   leave the row's test out, and report it with the Kiln `file:line`.
3. **No wildcard** (digest §13 obligation 2): every `match` over a Kiln
   enumeration lists every variant, no `_`, no `..` over variants, no
   `matches!`; a Kiln record or view is destructured without a rest
   pattern, so that a field Kiln adds does not compile.
4. **Properties** (WP-024 "Properties that are not negotiable"):
   determinism (no hash-map order, id, address or interning order in a
   stream); linear work; no panic on any AST Kiln can build (an
   unexpected shape is `Unaccounted`, not `unwrap`, not indexing);
   recursion bounded as `lib.rs` "What bounds the stack" says.
5. **Tests.** Every row of your tables gets the directory §1.5 names
   (`differ`, `equal`, `demand` or `indeterminate`), in the form
   `docs/wp/WP-024.md` "The pair tests" describes; the names leave
   `tests/tables/untested.txt` with `SLIP_BLESS=1 cargo test -p
   slip_digest --test tables`. A `differ` test changes one fact and
   names the unit that must change; choose sources in which the fact is
   visible while the other areas are pending. Where it cannot be (the
   difference is inside what another area still writes as its
   placeholder), write the test anyway and mark it: `expect.txt` gets
   the lines `# pending` and `# needs: <area>` (for an `equal` test, an
   `expect.txt` holding only those two lines); the runner skips it and
   the last area package runs them all. Keep these few and list their
   count by area in the handback. A row marked `unbuilt` needs no test.
   Rows that C++ source cannot reach through Kiln (say why, with the
   Kiln site) stay in `untested.txt` and are listed in the handback.
6. **Tests that can fail.** For every table, break at least one row in
   five on purpose (skip it, swap two, write a constant) and see its
   test fail, and do so for every row whose rule is in prose under a
   table. The handback lists the rows checked this way. Do not leave
   the breakage in.
7. **What the spec left open** goes to
   `spec/landed/digest-kinds/WP-031.md` (a decision per paragraph, the
   conservative one). Unit tests of helpers go in a `#[cfg(test)] mod
   tests` inside your own file, not in `src/tests.rs`.
8. **Gate.** `cargo xtask ci` (the full gate: this crate links Kiln)
   passes with `origin/main` merged in; `cargo test -p slip_digest`
   stays under a minute once built. Expect conflicts in
   `tests/tables/untested.txt` when merging `main`: resolve by taking
   `main`'s file and blessing again.

## Done when

The gate passes; every row of the tables in Scope has its test or is
listed in the handback with the reason; no function of your files
writes `pending` for your own area any more (say which pending calls
into other areas remain); the handback has what `docs/AGENTS.md` rule
12 asks, plus: rows implemented and rows left by table, the mutation
checks of rule 6, tests marked `# pending` by area, the functions added
for other areas, every row reported wrong, and the counters of
`digest.indeterminate` by cause over your own test sources.
