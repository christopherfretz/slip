## Scope

Three things `slip_cli` does for itself because the crates below it do
not (`docs/wp/WP-012.md`, Handback):

1. **Scratch entries of killed workers.** A worker killed for a cap, a
   time limit, a crash or a signal leaves `tmp/<pid>.…` in the store, and
   `spec/exec.md` §2 clears it only at the next exclusive open. The pool
   holds the exclusive store and knows which workers it killed: it
   removes their entries when it reaps them, and `cleanup_on_signal` does
   the same. `remove_dead_entries` in `slip_cli/src/commands.rs` goes.
   §2 "Cleaning" and §7 say so.
2. **Names of the model's enumerations.** `slip_model` exposes `name()`
   for `Reason`, `Status` and `End` (the names of `spec/model.md` §7 to
   §9, which its JSON rendering already has); the three tables in
   `commands.rs` go.
3. **The outcome of each observer.** `pool::Summary` gains, per selected
   observer in plan order, the final shard status and the first line of
   its message; `parse` stops reading every shard again.

## Tests

The tests of `crates/slip_cli/tests/cli.rs` pass unchanged. In
`crates/slip_exec/tests/pool.rs`: a worker killed for each reason leaves
no entry under `tmp/` once `run` returns; the summary's per-observer
outcomes against the shards in the store.
