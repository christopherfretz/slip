`crates/slip_cli`: the commands, options, summaries and exit statuses of
`spec/exec.md` §8, wiring `slip_cdb::plan::build` with `KilnCheck`,
`slip_exec::pool::run` with the worker command `slip __worker`, and
`slip_exec::worker::serve` with a `Task` around
`slip_kiln::parse_observer`. Option parsing is by hand (no dependency).

error.

The full brief (interface, tests, done-when) is written by the supervisor
when the work packages it needs have landed. Tests:
`crates/slip_cli/tests/cli.rs`, including each exit status, over a fixture
project.
