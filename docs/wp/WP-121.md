## Scope

The first run of Demo 1 on the corpus (2026-11-23, the preview from
`docs/wp/WP-059.md`'s branch) verified the control and failed every
observer of the relocation: 151 of 151 `failed`, with no reason. The
cause is known and was scheduled too late: the run's agreement
(`docs/wp/WP-070.md`, `slip_verify::run::Report::ours`) refuses an `ok`
shard of a run with rules that has no `binding` or no `rule_use`, and
the verify job writes neither. Their writing is item 3 of
`docs/wp/WP-074.md`, which waits for the overlay (`docs/wp/WP-066.md`,
Kiln's KF2). Nothing in the tree-form half of that item needs the
overlay. This package takes that half; WP-074 keeps the overlay and
`spellings`.

1. **`binding`** (`spec/verify.md` §9.1, section 24): in every shard the
   verify task writes when the job has a declared rule table that is not
   empty, whatever the parse's outcome (also an `unverifiable` and a
   `diagnosed` one), taken from `Job.post`: `overlay` absent, `declared`
   the content id the job was given, `pre` the round of the pre shard.
   A shard the coordinator writes for a worker that produced none has
   none (unchanged).
2. **`rule_use`** (section 25; `Options.rule_use`, `docs/wp/WP-069.md`):
   in every shard whose outcome is `ok` when the rule table is not
   empty: the post-image walk of step 1 runs with `rule_use: true`, and
   what it counted is the section. Say which parse's counts are written
   when the job parses the post-image twice (G3's second parse) and why
   they cannot differ; if they can, that is G3's failure, not a choice.
3. **M1 is byte for byte what it was**: a job with an empty rule table
   (no `--declared`, or one with no rule) writes neither section. Pin it
   with the existing goldens and one test that compares a shard's bytes
   before and after this package for a run without rules.
4. **The failure message.** A shard the run refuses as "not this run's"
   reaches the user as `slip: failed: <main file>: ` with nothing after
   the colon. Find where the reason is lost (`Report::add`, the text of
   §7.1) and make the line say why (`the shard has no rule_use`, `the
   shard's binding is not this run's`, …): a reason the report decides,
   in `slip_verify::run`, with a test per cause. If that changes a
   golden of the report for a run that fails today, list the goldens.
5. **End to end**, through the executable (`crates/slip_cli/tests/verify.rs`):
   a relocation with `slip declare` and `slip verify` over two
   observers: every observer `verified`, the run's verdict what
   `spec/verify.md` §9.2 gives a namespace rule today (the rule is
   listed `unchecked` until `docs/wp/WP-111.md`; the verdict is not
   changed by it): state the exit status and pin it. A rename (a rule
   of another kind) with one observer left stale: the `stale` finding.
   The wrong edit beside each: `mismatch`.
   `s2_1_rule_naming_a_dependency_entity_matches_nothing` pins status 4
   today for a reason that goes away: say what it gives now and why
   that is right.

## Handback

**Built.** The verify job writes `binding` (every shard it returns) and
`rule_use` (every `ok` shard, from step 1's walk with `rule_use` on)
when `Declared.rules` is not empty, and neither when it is
(`crates/slip_cli/src/verify_job.rs`; `Params` gains `declared_id`, set
by the worker's `make_params` from `Job.post.declared`). The report's
refusal of a shard as not this run's now carries a reason, which is the
observer's message and its line on standard error
(`crates/slip_verify/src/run.rs`: `ours` returns `Result<(), String>`,
`read_sections` the fold's refusal). Details, the answers to items 2, 4
and 5, and the mutations: `spec/landed/verify/WP-121.md`.

**Decisions.** `rule_use.rules` is `Declared::rules_id()` (the verdict's
and the header's id, not `rules().id()`). The binding is put on the
shard after step 1's parse, so the `diagnosed`/`rejected` early return
carries it too. Item 3's "before and after" is a golden
(`crates/slip_cli/tests/golden/verify-no-rules.txt`) blessed on main's
code in its own commit before the job changed, with path-, Kiln- and
walk-dependent parts as placeholders or left out. The messages are the
WP's examples, completed per cause (landed §4). The test
`s7_a_foreign_shard_has_no_message` (WP-055) pinned the empty message
and is replaced by `s7_1_a_foreign_shard_says_why`; no golden moved.

**Owed after WP-053 merges.** Item 5 through the executable
(`crates/slip_cli/tests/verify.rs`): the relocation (status 0), the
rename with a stale observer (status 1), the wrong edits (status 1),
and `s2_1_rule_naming_a_dependency_entity_matches_nothing`, which then
gives status 1 (both `mismatch`, rule unused) instead of its pinned 4.
The job-level cases are in `crates/slip_cli/tests/verify_job.rs`.
