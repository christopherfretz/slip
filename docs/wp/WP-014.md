# WP-014 — M0 exit: the first LLVM baseline

- Status: done
- Milestone: M0
- Track: G
- Needs: WP-011, WP-012, WP-013
- Size: M

## Scope

Not an implementation package: the supervisor runs it. `slip plan` and
`slip parse` over the `llvm` corpus (`cargo xtask corpus path llvm --db`)
within the memory budget of this host, then:

- every excluded entry and every shard that is not `ok` classified as a
  Slip defect (fixed, or filed as a work package), a Kiln defect (reduced
  and reported to the Kiln agent), or a property of the corpus (recorded in
  `bench/corpora.toml` or the spec that should say so);
- `cargo xtask perf record llvm <run record>` committed as
  `bench/baselines/llvm.json`, with `perf summarize`'s output in the
  handback;
- a second run compared with `perf check` (counters exact) and its shards
  compared byte for byte with the first run's (`spec/model.md` §8: a shard
  is deterministic);
- runs at `-j` 1, 4 and 8 on a slice (`--limit`) to see how wall time and
  `peak_rss_sum` scale, and one full run: reported to the operator with
  the measured per-observer and summed peaks and what a 32 GiB limit would
  change;
- every `spec/landed/` file folded into its spec, and `round` added to
  `Run`, as the close of M0.

## Done when

The baseline is committed, a repeated run passes `perf check` with
identical shards, and nothing is unclassified.

## Out of scope

libc++'s test suite as a corpus (a later package). Digests (M1).

## Handback

Run by the supervisor on 2026-11-22: LLVM 19.1.7 (`cargo xtask corpus`),
Kiln `4bfd999da`, a 24-core container with no memory limit of its own
under a 31 GiB VM, 12 jobserver tokens. The baseline is
`bench/baselines/llvm.json`.

**Plan.** 4069 entries, 4061 observers (eight files are compiled twice
and merge), nothing excluded; 1.6 s.

**The baseline pair** (`slip parse --db … -j 12`, two fresh stores):

| | wall s | CPU s | efficiency | peak max | p99 | p90 | p50 | summed peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| recorded | 746.1 | 6763.8 | 0.76 | 459 MB | 285 MB | 206 MB | 89 MB | 2.14 GB |
| repeat | 721.6 | 6512.7 | 0.75 | 458 MB | 284 MB | 205 MB | 89 MB | 2.22 GB |

Both: 4053 `ok`, 2 `diagnosed`, 6 `failed` (12 crashed jobs: each of
the six, and its one retry), nothing killed for memory or time;
`inputs.files` 1 836 485, `inputs.bytes` 32 412 847 921. `perf check`
of the repeat against the record ends `perf check: llvm: ok`, and the
4061 shards of the two stores are byte for byte the same. The largest
unit by time and by memory is
`clang/unittests/StaticAnalyzer/RangeSetTest.cpp` (8.8 s, 459 MB).

**Scaling**, on the 240 largest units (`--limit 240`):

| `-j` | wall s | CPU s | summed peak | per-unit peak max | p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 699.7 | 659.6 | 0.39 GB | 375–393 MB | 154–161 MB |
| 4 | 209.8 | 733.3 | 1.00 GB | | |
| 8 | 105.4 | 703.5 | 1.63 GB | | |

Counters and shards are the same at every width. A running worker
costs about 0.2 GB, so memory does not bound the width on this host or
on any host with more than a quarter of a gigabyte per core; a 32 GiB
limit changes nothing at 12 workers, and 24 would fit as well. What
bounds the run is cores: the efficiency of 0.75 at `-j 12` is CPU time
over 12 × wall, the rest being the coordinator's serial part and
start-up of each worker.

**Classification** (`spec/harness.md` §8.7): no Slip defect. Two
`diagnosed` units are the corpus's own (clang 19 rejects them with the
same error). Six `failed` units are two panics of Kiln's constant
evaluator, reduced to a few lines each and reported
(`docs/requests/KD1-oven-panics-llvm.md`; Kiln's WP-2060).

**The close of M0.** Every `spec/landed/` file is folded into its spec;
`Run` carries its round (WP-019); the CLI's workarounds are gone
(WP-018).
