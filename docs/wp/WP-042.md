## Scope

`docs/requests/KQ1-digest-fields.md` §E: Kiln's `kiln_abi::odr_def`
hashes a definition for the link-time one-definition-rule check; Slip's
digest hashes a unit for verification. Both read Kiln's syntax tree. A
fact that one reads and the other does not is, each time, one of: a
reason the purposes differ, a gap of Slip's, or a gap of Kiln's. This
package finds every such fact and says which.

1. **Read `odr_def` in full**, in Kiln at Slip's pin (`kiln/` of the
   checkout): `kiln/crates/kiln_abi/src/odr_def/` (eleven files, 4235
   lines: `mod.rs`, `signature.rs`, `lambda.rs`, `expr.rs`, `refs.rs`,
   `decl.rs`, `init.rs`, `misc.rs`, `attrs.rs`, `stmt.rs`,
   `internal.rs`) and `kiln/crates/kiln_abi/src/odr.rs` (689 lines), with
   its contract, `kiln/spec/abi.md` "Definition hash (`odr_def`)"
   (`abi.md:2202`).
2. **The table**, in `spec/landed/digest-kinds/WP-042.md`: one line per
   fact `odr_def` reads (below).
3. **The findings**, in the same file: every line that is not `same`,
   grouped by verdict, each with its evidence.
4. **A draft reply to Kiln**, `docs/requests/KQ1-amendment-1.md`, in the
   form of the other files of `docs/requests/` (status **draft**; the
   supervisor sends it): what the audit found that may be a gap on
   Kiln's side, each with the `file:line` in Kiln and a source of a few
   lines that shows two definitions `odr_def` hashes alike and that
   differ in meaning, or says that none was found.

Files you own: `spec/landed/digest-kinds/WP-042.md`,
`docs/requests/KQ1-amendment-1.md`. Nothing else: `spec/digest.md` and
`spec/digest-kinds.md` are not edited here; what you would change in

## The table

One line per **read** of the syntax tree in `odr_def`: a field of a node,
a variant of a kind enumeration it matches on, a query of `Ast` or of the
source manager.

| Column | What |
|---|---|
| Where | `file:line` in Kiln at the pin |
| Fact | the descriptor and member, as `spec/digest-kinds.md` §1.2 writes a row (`FunctionData.spec-inline`, `IfStmt.aux-kind`), or the query's name |
| `odr_def` | written, or read to decide something (say what) |
| Row | the row of `spec/digest-kinds.md` that covers the fact, with its decision (`in`, `out`, `symbolic`, `demand`, `unsupported`) |
| Verdict | below |

Verdicts:

- `same`: the row is `in` (or `symbolic`, `demand`) and `odr_def` writes
  the fact; or both leave it out.
- `slip-only-out`: `odr_def` writes it and the row is `out`. **Every one
  is read against the row's note.** Either the note's reason holds for
  verification (say why in a line: the fact is stored twice and the
  other row carries it; it is a spelling and the digest is of meaning),
  or it does not, and the line is a **finding against Slip**: a fact
  that distinguishes two definitions for the linker and that the digest
  ignores.
- `slip-unsupported`: the row is `unsupported`. Not a finding (the unit
  is indeterminate), but listed: these are rows M1's exit must decide
  (`spec/harness.md` §9.2).
- `no-row`: `odr_def` reads a fact no row covers (a query, a side table
  of `Ast`, a derived fact). Say which stream of the digest carries the
  same information, with the section of `spec/digest.md`, or that none
  does: a **finding against Slip**.
- `kiln-only-out`: the row is `in` and `odr_def` does not read the fact
  at all. Found from the other side: for each table of
  `spec/digest-kinds.md` whose descriptor `odr_def` visits (functions,
  variables, statements, expressions, the declarations a body can hold,
  attributes, lambdas, initialisers), every `in` row that no line above
  mentions. Either the fact cannot differ between two definitions that
  the linker would merge (say why), or it can: a **possible gap of
  Kiln's**, for the reply.

Do not sample. The audit is worth what its coverage is: the landed file
states, per file of `odr_def`, the number of lines of the table and that
the file was read to its end, and for the reverse direction the tables
that were gone through.

## What differs by purpose, and is not a finding

Say it once at the head of the landed file, with the section of
`kiln/spec/abi.md` or `spec/digest.md` that fixes each, and then use it
as the reason on the lines it explains:

- `odr_def` hashes a **definition** the linker may merge (inline
  functions, templates' instantiations, variables); the digest has a unit
  for every entity, defined or not. A fact of declarations only is out of
  `odr_def`'s reach by design.
- `odr_def` names entities for the linker (mangled names, `AnonIds`);
  the digest names them by key (`spec/digest.md` §3). The two are
  compared by **what distinguishes two entities**, not by spelling.
- `odr_def` may be token-like where the standard's rule is ("each
  definition shall consist of the same sequence of tokens"); the digest
  is of resolved meaning. A written form `odr_def` keeps and the digest
  drops is expected; a **resolved** fact `odr_def` keeps and the digest
  drops is not.

## Tests

None run. The table is checked by the reviewer on a sample of twenty
lines drawn across the files, each followed from Kiln's line to the row,
and on every line marked as a finding.

## Done when

- `cargo xtask ci --quick` passes with `origin/main` merged in (the
  lints read the two files).
- `spec/landed/digest-kinds/WP-042.md` has the head, the table, the
  coverage statement, and the findings by verdict.
- Each **finding against Slip** has: the row or the missing stream; a
  source of a few lines with two forms that differ in meaning; what
  `slip_digest` gives for them at your base, checked with a pair test
  run locally (`crates/slip_digest/tests/differ/` is the form; the pair
  is **given in the landed file, not added to the tree**, since the
  test directories belong to the digest's open packages); and the
  proposed row. A finding that the digest already catches through
  another row is closed there, with that row.
- Each **possible gap of Kiln's** is in the draft reply with its source.
  If there is none, the reply says so in two lines and lists what was
  compared.
- The handback gives the counts by verdict and the three findings you
  judge most serious, in a paragraph each.

## Out of scope

Changing `spec/digest-kinds.md`, `spec/digest.md` or any code (each
finding becomes a row or a package of the digest's, written by the
supervisor); `kiln_abi`'s layout serialization and mangling
(`odr.rs` is read for what it feeds `odr_def` and for the kinds of
record, not audited as a hasher of layouts); Kiln's main beyond the pin
(note in the reply's head which commit was read; if `odr_def` changed on
Kiln's main since, say so and stop there).

Reviewed as the trusted base's documents are: the question is whether a
line was skipped.
