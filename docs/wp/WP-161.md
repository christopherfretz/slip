# WP-161 — the store without link-then-unlink, and reads by id that are checked

- Status: todo
- Milestone: M1
- Track: C
- Needs: none
- Size: S

## Scope

Found by WP-159 (`spec/landed/harness/WP-159.md`, "A finding" and the
audit; the supervisor's rulings under `docs/wp/WP-159.md`).

1. **No promise of the store rests on reading a name just after it was
   linked and its source unlinked.** On the workspace mount (`cargo
   xtask fscheck`, row `unique-tmp-link`) such a name reads as absent
   for a while, about once in 1,000 to 6,000. The two sequences that do
   it are the lock (`slip_exec::store::Store::take_lock`) and the
   journal (`slip_cli::transform::store::StoreJournal::publish`).
   Ruled: both are made by **exclusive creation of the final name**
   (`create_new`), written and synced in place; no hard link is left in
   the product.
   - The lock: a reader that finds it empty or without its newline reads
     "being written" and retries as it retries a lost race today; the
     take-over of a stale lock keeps its check that what was moved aside
     is the stale lock. Say what a crash between creation and the write
     leaves (an empty lock whose owner cannot be read) and how the next
     opener gets past it without two openers taking it at once; if that
     cannot be made sound without a link, stop and say so with the
     sequence that breaks.
   - The journal: `slip_apply` touches no file of the tree before the
     journal is written and synced (check this in `slip_apply::write`
     and state where). A journal that does not decode whole (its length
     and digest are in it; if they are not, the format gains them by its
     version rule) is "the write was interrupted before any file was
     touched": `apply --recover` removes it and says so; `apply --write`
     refuses as it does for any journal.
   - The close of a store that reads its lock as absent, and a process
     that opens the same store again after such a close (today: its own
     live pid in the lock, `Busy`), are tests.
   - `cargo xtask fscheck` gains the row for the new sequence
     (`create-new-read`, relied on) and `unique-tmp-link` becomes "not
     relied on"; `spec/harness.md` §11 and `spec/exec.md` §2.1 follow.
2. **A read by id is checked against the id.** The plan read by `--plan
   <id>` and the declared changes read by `check --declared <id>` are
   decoded and not compared with the id they were asked by
   (`spec/exec.md` §2.1 "Which reads are checked"). Both are compared;
   a difference is the `Input` error a blob gives. The map shards `slip
   map` counts failed runs in, and run records, stay as they are: say in
   §2.1 why each cannot mislead (what is compared instead).

## Tests

For item 1: the lock and the journal over a fake that makes a created
name read as absent for the next `n` reads (the shape of
`slip_apply`'s fake file system), and over the real one; two openers
racing for a stale lock (the existing test, unchanged in what it
asserts); a crash after creation and before the write, for the lock and
for the journal; the mutations "the reader takes an empty lock as free"
and "recover reads a short journal as nothing to recover and leaves it".
For item 2: a plan and declared changes stored under another's id, each
refused by every command that reads one by id.

link count.
