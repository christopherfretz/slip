## What is decided

As WP-163's "What is decided" 1 to 5, unchanged, except that Kiln is
active. A test that fails at the new pin because Kiln is wrong becomes
`xfail` with a minimal reproduction, and goes in the handback's "For
Kiln" for the supervisor to send. If more than a handful fail, or one
is on the rename's path, stop and hand back.
