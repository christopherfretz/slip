# WP-071 — slip_refactor: the native rename of functions and variables (core)

`crates/slip_refactor` (`spec/edits.md` §9, the part `docs/wp/WP-071.md`
puts in scope): `src/lib.rs` (`run`, `run_counted`), `src/rename/`
(`mod.rs`, `target.rs`, `subjects.rs`, `claims.rs`, `decls.rs`,
`refs.rs`, and the four conservative hooks `families.rs`,
`followers.rs`, `spec_members.rs`, `dependent.rs`), and
`tests/rename.rs` with 55 directories under `tests/rename/`. Kiln sites
are at Slip's pin `913a490bb`, under `kiln/crates/`.

## What was built

- **`run`** (the §9.7 signature) and **`run_counted`**, which also gives
  the map shard's `edits.*` counters (§6.3), sorted by name:
  `edits.changed`, `edits.claims`, `edits.claims.{keep, refuse, replace,
  unresolved}`, `edits.files`, `edits.groups`, `edits.links`,
  `edits.refs` (the written references the pass looked at, "Cost"),
  `edits.reports`, `edits.subjects`.
- **The order of a run**: `target::seeds` (one pass over `decl_names`,
  one over `written_refs`); the hooks `families`, `followers`,
  `spec_members`; the flags `unreachable` and `name_taken`; the claims of
  declarations (`decls`), of references (`refs`), of dependent names
  (`dependent`); `Claims::finish`, `Subjects::finish`,
  `Edits::normalise`.
- **`Claims`** holds one derivation per span Kiln gave and stance; two
  entries of one span are joined (subjects united, the written forms
  joined). `finish` decides the piece of a range over every `replace`
  derivation that lands there (one spelled key: `spelled`; anything else:
  `literal`) and gives each derivation one vote; `normalise` joins them.
- **`Subjects`**, by `KeyId`; `add` takes the entity through
  `Keys::entity`, the id from `Keys::key_id`, the rule from
  `Keys::rename_rule` (an `Err` there: `unsupported`).

## Decided where the spec was silent

1. **`Err` from `run`**, all failures of Slip: a request that is not a
   rename, or has no rename record; a request `Transform::validate`
   rejects; no content id; a unit parsed without `ParseOptions::written`;
   a unit without a main file; a claim in a buffer `files` does not
   know.
2. **New names.** `Transform::validate` is called first, so a new name
   that is not `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*` (a UCN, `café`) or is a
   keyword (`delete`) makes `run` return `Err` ("the request is not
   valid: …") and never reaches `Keys::rename_rule`. A reserved
   identifier (`_x`, `__y`) is accepted as `validate` accepts it. A new
   name that is the target's last component is `validate`'s error too.
   Test: `requests_the_rename_does_not_run`.
3. **`Ctx` additions**: `named` (the `decl_names` entries named like the
   old and like the new name, from the one pass) and `settled` (the
   declarations a hook dealt with, which `decls::claim` skips). The
   fixed fields are as `docs/wp/WP-071.md` gives them.
4. **Derivations are keyed by Kiln's span and the stance**: one span
   with two `written` entries is one derivation; two spans at one range
   (an argument substituted twice, a header included twice) are two
   votes. A span with derivations of two stances (a macro argument that
   denotes a subject in one substitution and something else in the
   other) gives two claims at one range, a `replace` and a `keep`, which
   reduce sees as such (`s9_3-macro-argument-mixed`).
5. **Another kind is read literally** (§9.1): an entity of the target's
   name and scope whose key kind is not allowed (a variable under
   `kind: function`, a struct) is a seed with `unsupported`
   (`s9_1-kind-variable-not-function`, `s9_1-another-kind`).
6. **C language linkage per declaration**: an entity with key parent
   `c_linkage` is a seed with `unsupported` when the declaration it was
   reached by, or its origin, has the target's scopes; otherwise it is no
   seed and its names are `keep`s (`s9_1-c-linkage-in-target`,
   `s9_1-c-linkage-elsewhere`).
7. **`read_only` and a refusal.** A declaration of a subject whose name
   `sites` refuses `not_owned` sets `read_only` and is not claimed. One
   refused for an earlier reason (`macro_body`) at a place in a file that
   is not owned sets `read_only` and is claimed `refuse` as well: the
   refusal is what `sites` says about that token.
8. **Dependent names** (the conservative `dependent.rs`) are claimed
   `unresolved` when `sites` locates the token with the old name's
   spelling: a dependent entry has no referent (K4), so this is the one
   place the rename asks `sites` about a name the AST did not name
   first. Its cost is one `name_token` per dependent entry of the unit.
   A dependent using-declaration (`using B<T>::f;`) is claimed
   `unresolved` the same way.
9. **Labels** get nothing: neither of Kiln's tables records a label or a
   `goto`'s name, so nothing is claimed there
   (`s4_2-locals-parameters-fields-labels`).
10. **Names of declarations** (`decls::name_of`): its identifier; a
    constructor's or destructor's, its class's; a deduction guide's, its
    template's; `None` for an operator, a conversion function and a
    literal operator. An inheriting constructor's using-declaration is
    named by Kiln as a constructor of the class that declares it, so it
    is given that class's name. The request cannot name an operator, a
    conversion or a literal operator (`operator` is a keyword, so
    `validate` rejects it); a literal operator `operator""_x` is no seed
    of a rename of `_x`, its key name not being `identifier(_x)`
    (`s9_1-literal-operator-not-named`). No `crossing` refusal arises
    from them in this package; it would come with WP-072's links.
11. **`name_taken`** is decided from `decl_names` only: the entities
    named like the new name whose enclosing scope is the subject's
    scope, or, for a class, one of its bases at any depth (through each
    class's definition). Bases reached only through a dependent base or
    a specialisation the observer does not complete are missed; that is
    safe (verification decides).
12. **Hooks run on seeds** (`families`, `spec_members`, the friend
    followers), except `unnamed_types` and the using-declarations, which
    look at every subject.
13. **`unreachable` through `redecls_of`.** `ast.templates.specs_of`
    lists a specialisation's record; the declarations written are its
    redeclarations. An explicit instantiation is hidden when none of its
    declarations has an extent row of kind `ExplicitInstantiation` (K1);
    an explicit specialisation when one of its declarations has a row and
    no `decl_names` entry, or none of them has an entry (a name a macro
    spells, below).
14. **Friends in a class template's pattern** (`docs/wp/WP-071.md`,
    "Added after the review of WP-103"). The friend declaration's key
    has the template as parent and its own discriminator, so it is
    matched to the seeds by scope (its enclosing entity is the seed's),
    kind and name, not by discriminator; it follows every seed it
    matches (every function of that name in that scope is a seed) and
    has its own rule. **Where the friend is also defined in the
    template**, the digest makes the function's key follow the pattern
    (`spec/landed/digest/WP-103.md`, finding 2), and a rule on the
    function's own key makes it indeterminate
    (`key/rule-on-instantiated-member`, which the post-image test now
    fails on). So the subject whose key follows a pattern carries no rule
    (`rule` absent), and the friend's rule is the one that renames it;
    a subject that follows a pattern that is not a subject would be
    `unsupported` (no case reaches it). This reads the addendum's "the
    friend also defined in the template (which is followed and needs no
    rule of its own)" as the digest decides it: the friend is followed
    by the function, and the function needs no rule of its own
    (`wp103-friend-defined-in-template`).
15. **The post-image test** (`rules_give_the_ids_in_the_post_image`)
    runs on every directory whose claims are all `replace` or `keep`,
    with no range claimed twice, and which has a subject with a rule and
    no flag. It splices the `replace` claims, parses the post-image,
    walks it under the rules of every subject, and asserts that each
    such subject's id is the key id of a declaration named like the new
    name, and that no declaration named like the new name has an
    indeterminate key.
16. **`Spelled` keys** are `Keys::key_id(Keys::entity(written::origin(
    referent)))`, which is the subject's id by construction; a token
    whose derivations give two keys, or a spelled and a literal, is
    written `literal`.
17. **The rendering of `expect.txt`** gives reasons by their wire names
    (`macro_body`, `not_owned`, …).

## Found in Slip

- **A specialisation whose name a macro spells** is absent from
  `written::decl_names` (`crates/slip_kiln/src/written.rs:399`,
  `identifier_span`: the token at the name's start is the macro's name,
  not the identifier). Smallest source:
  ```cpp
  #define NAME f
  namespace n {
  template <class T> void f(T) {}
  template <> void NAME<int>(int) {}
  }
  ```
  The rename flags `n::f` `unreachable`
  (`known-macro-named-explicit-specialisation`). A specialisation a
  macro body writes whole is given and refused `macro_body`
  (`tiers-explicit-specialisation-in-macro-body`), not absent.
- **A declaration with a line splice or a UCN in its name** is given and
  refused `not_plain` (`known-line-splice-declaration`), as expected.

## Notes for the next packages

- **WP-072** (families, followers). Replace `families.rs` and the first
  two items of `followers.rs`; keep `friends_in_patterns` and
  `followed_patterns`, or move them with their tests (the three `wp103-*`
  directories). `Subjects::link` exists and is unused. A follower is
  added with `Subjects::add(…, Role::Follower, …, &of)`; joining keeps
  the least role and unites `of`. A follower whose rule `Keys` refuses
  gets `unsupported` from `add` itself. `ctx.settled` is how a hook keeps
  `decls::claim` from claiming a declaration it has dealt with (a
  using-declaration's name becomes a `replace literal` there).
- **WP-073** (dependent names, members of class templates).
  `Claims::refuse` takes `mixed_set` and `mixed_instances` and is unused
  today. `dependent.rs` is called once per `written::dependent_names`
  entry; the `changed` entries go in `ctx.changed`. `spec_members.rs`
  flags every seed enclosed by a class template; it is also what keeps
  members of instantiations out of `followed_patterns` today.
- **WP-074** (the worker). Call `run_counted` and put its counters on the
  shard; `files` is `Sites::file_ref`. `run` returns `Err` only for a
  failure of Slip; a diagnosed unit is not given to it (a call on one
  does not panic). `keys` must be a `Keys` with no rules over the same
  `with_input` as the unit. The transform id is
  `content_id(transform.to_bytes())`.
