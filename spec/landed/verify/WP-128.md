# WP-128 — the place of a finding for a derived unit on the pre side

Code: `crates/slip_digest/src/trace.rs` (`entity_place`, the new
`made_before_explicit_specialisation`). The verify job's localisation
(`crates/slip_cli/src/locate.rs`, `fill`/`place`) takes a site from the
traces' marks and was not changed; `crates/slip_cli/src/verify_job.rs` is
untouched. Tests: `crates/slip_digest/tests/trace.rs` (`s6_1_place_of_*`),
`crates/slip_cli/tests/locate.rs` (`s6_1_explicit_specialisation_removed`),
the pitfall catalogue (E11 `bad-3`).

## 1. What §6.1 says, and which was wrong

`spec/verify.md` §6.1, "A unit's place": the place is "that of the first
declaration of its chain, in chain order, that the source wrote. A
declaration the front end made, by instantiation or implicitly, has no
place of its own and is skipped, also when it comes first in the chain: an
explicit specialisation `template <> int pick<int>()` in `pick_int.h`
whose chain begins with the declaration Kiln instantiated from the
template in `pick.h` has its place in `pick_int.h`. When no declaration of
the chain is written (a derived unit, an implicit member), the place is
that of the first declaration of the chain that has a location, which for
an instantiation is where its pattern is".

The text is clear, and names E11 `bad-3`'s case: the pre side of
`changed pick<int>()` (declared in the pre-image, derived in the post) is
`pick_int.h:1`, the post side `pick.h:1`. The code was wrong. Kiln makes a
declaration of `pick<int>` at the pattern when it deduces the
specialisation, and when the source then specialises it Kiln sets the
`SpecInfo` that declaration shares with the written one to
`ExplicitSpecialization` and adds the written declaration after it
(`kiln_sema` `act_on_explicit_function_spec`). `templ::made_by_instantiation`
reads that kind and, for a non-member, a lexical context that is no
instantiation, and called the made declaration written. A member of an
implicit specialisation explicitly specialised (`template <> int
S<long>::m()`) was already right: its made declaration's lexical context
is the instantiated class.

The fix: a function's first declaration whose specialisation kind is
`ExplicitSpecialization` and that a written declaration follows is not
written: Kiln's own reading of "first written" (`Ast::contract_first`).
Only places move; no digest, verdict, state or count (a place is read
from the chain after the walk, `verify.md` §5.2).

E22 `bad-7` is no second case: `make()` is a declared unit on both sides,
placed by the site of the difference (corrected to line 9 in WP-056), and
it passes.

## 3. Mutation

"The place taken from the pattern": in `entity_place`, no declaration
counts as written, so every place is the first declaration of the chain
with a location. It fails three tests and nothing else of
`slip_digest --test trace`, `slip_cli --test locate` and `--test pitfalls`:

- `trace.rs` `s6_1_place_of_an_explicit_specialisation`: `pick<int>()`
  before the edit at line 1, expected 3;
- `locate.rs` `s6_1_explicit_specialisation_removed`: `where.pre` at line
  1, expected 3;
- the catalogue: `FAIL e11-include-removed/bad-3 … [changed pick<int>() @
  pick.h:1]` (91 rows, 311 trees: PASS 305 XFAIL 5 FAIL 1).

The other rows of the table hold under it: their places are already the
first declaration with a location (an explicitly defaulted member is its
own first declaration).

## 4. The catalogue

`crates/slip_verify/tests/pitfalls/e11-include-removed/expect.txt`: the
line `xfail bad-3: place, WP-128: …` is removed; `bad-3` passes with places
mandatory.

## 5. Open point

§6.1's gloss "which for an instantiation is where its pattern is" is not
true of a class template's explicit instantiation that is the first to
name the specialisation: the place is the directive, as the rule ("the
first declaration of the chain that has a location") gives it, because
Kiln makes the declaration there. Rule and code agree; the gloss could
say "for an implicit instantiation". Left to the supervisor (not changed).
