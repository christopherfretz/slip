# WP-108 — §4 step 4: declared in one image, derived in the other

What `crates/slip_verify/src/compare.rs` does for `spec/verify.md` §4
step 4, where building showed §4 needed a sentence, and the attempts of
item 3 to break the amended rule, each with its outcome.

## Step 4 as built

- `compare_groups` collects the derived multiset by key first (each key
  of `Vp` or `Vq` with its two runs), then walks `D` (steps 1, 2 and 4),
  then the derived keys (step 3).
- In the walk of `D`, after step 2: a key whose step-2 kind is `removed`
  becomes `changed` when `Vq(k) − Vp(k)` is not empty; one whose kind is
  `added` when `Vp(k) − Vq(k)` is not empty (multisets of digests, an
  indeterminate unit counted like any other). The `changed` list alone can
  then prevent it (and is then used); `removed` and `created` are not
  consulted for it. The key is remembered.
- In step 3 a remembered key is not counted in `only_pre` or `only_post`.
  Its `derived_indeterminate` or `derived_changed`, if step 3 gives one,
  stands beside the `changed`.
- A key indeterminate in `D` is step 1's alone: step 4 does not apply to
  it, so its derived unit on the other side is counted by step 3 as
  before (the `indeterminate` finding already makes the observer not
  verified).
- The finding is made as every finding about `D` is (§6): `pre` from
  `R(k)` and `post` from `A(k)` of `D`, so exactly one of them is
  present; `file` from the declared or `tu` group that differs. Nothing
  in the `Finding` says it came from step 4: localisation (§6.1, the row
  for step 4) can tell it by the key having a declared unit in one image
  and a derived unit in the other.
- **The place of a step-4 finding is not its `file`** (supervisor's
  ruling, `spec/verify.md` §6.1 "place"). `file` is the unit's group:
  for `bad-3`'s `pick<int>()` that is `pick.h`, because the chain of the
  explicit specialisation begins with the declaration Kiln instantiated
  from the template there (`units.rs` `located(representative(e))`).
  The place, `@ file:line`, is that of the first declaration the source
  wrote, skipping those Kiln made: `pick_int.h:1` (and `spec.h:1` for
  `bad`'s `traits<Mine>`). Localisation, not built yet, must read the
  place from the chain and must not take it from `file`.

## Why it is read on the differences of `V` (§4 amended)

Read literally, "a key that has a unit in `Vq`" is a presence question,
and §4.1 warns against exactly that. With a key that has, in the
pre-image, a declared unit and also a derived unit, and in the
post-image only that derived unit, unchanged in its group, the full
comparison would say `changed` and the comparison through `G` (which
leaves the unchanged derived group out) `removed`.
`tests/groups.rs` `s4_1_through_groups_equals_over_all_units` finds such
a pair (mutation M5, "Mutations" below). So step 4 is stated on `Vq(k) − Vp(k)`, which
leaving out the groups outside `G` does not change (§4.1's own
argument). On what the walk produces the two readings agree: a second
entity of one key in one image is a collision, every unit of the key is
then indeterminate (`digest.md` §3.4, §10), and step 1 takes the key; so
where step 2 gives `removed`, `Vp(k)` is empty. `spec/verify.md` §4
step 4 now says this, with the two runs `collides` does not mark
(`crates/slip_digest/src/units.rs`): a run of namespaces only (a
namespace is always in the `tu` group, so never in `V`; Kiln merges a
written `namespace std` with its implicit one into one unit, as the
review's probes showed), and a run of repeatable kinds with equal
digests (content-keyed under their parent; a derived one has an implicit
instantiation for its parent, which no declared unit has). Were either
ever in `D` and `V` of one image, the reading on the differences still
holds through groups and reports nothing for a derived unit both images
have equally.

Step 1 comes before step 4: a key with an indeterminate declared unit
gets `indeterminate`, which no exemption prevents, even where the other
image derives the key and `changed` is declared
(`tests/compare.rs` `s4_4_step_1_comes_first`; the review's R1 put step
4 and its exemption first and turned such an observer `verified`).

## Through groups (§4.1)

The group shortcut cannot skip a key of step 4. Step 2 has a finding for
it, so a declared or `tu` group that holds it differs between the images
and is in `G`. The other image's `V` holds a unit of the key that this
image's does not, so a derived group holding that unit differs too and is
in `G`. The derived groups of `G` are opened (their unit lists given to
`compare_groups`) for this as for step 3: to tell `removed` (or `added`)
from `changed`. `tests/groups.rs` `s4_4_through_groups` has the
declared unit in a header's group and the derived unit in the
translation unit's, in both directions, with the exemptions, the derived
unit indeterminate, and a multiplicity with one copy in a header group
that is otherwise unchanged; the property test counts the pairs with a
step-4 finding (1320 of 12000) and those where a presence reading would
differ (36).

**What a partial specialisation's instantiation records (attempt 3).**
`S<int *>` is derived on both sides, from two patterns. Its own unit is
the same on both sides today: the pattern chosen for a class is in the
resolved unit `definition` (digest §2.2), which is `Pending(Demand)`.
Its members' units differ. So a real difference (attempt 3, 3d) is
reported through the members and is not lost.

**Adding `#include <new>` (7, 7b) and builtins.** An edit that makes a
translation unit declare `operator new` (or `operator delete`,
`std::align_val_t`) where the pre-image used Kiln's implicit
declaration, and referenced it, must declare `changed` for each such
entity (`changed operator new(unsigned long)`), and `created` for the
ones the pre-image did not reference. `slip declare` resolves the
`changed` line in the pre-image, whose records include the derived unit.
Acceptable: it is declarable, it names what the include brings into the
translation unit, and a shared header reached by observers that
referenced the entity and observers that did not is declared with both
lines (an exemption one observer does not need is not an error, §2.2).
What it costs: `changed` lets the entity's unit differ in any way, so a
`<new>` that declared `operator new` with another exception
specification would not be reported through that unit; a caller whose
own stream depends on it still is. A builtin that a source redeclares
under its own name (7e) has a declared unit on both sides; a library
function declared where only its `__builtin_` form was used (7d) is
another entity (`created`); `__int128_t` (7c) is a type, written
canonically, and gets no unit until declared (`created`).

**Ownership (§2.1).** No attempt above uses rules. Step 4 relies on the
explicit specialisation and the instantiation having one key under
rules too; §2.1 "Rules rename owned entities only" says that a
specialisation takes its template's ownership for exactly that. An
attempt with a rule table (an owned explicit specialisation of a
dependency template under `acme => legacy`) depends on that sentence,
which is not implemented yet (WP-110); it is not tried here.
