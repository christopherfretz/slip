# WP-055 — records, declared changes by name, the run's report, the order of findings

What `slip_digest::records`, `slip_cli::declare`, `slip_verify::run` and the
order of findings read into `spec/verify.md` where it is silent.

## The order of findings (§6)

- `FindingKind::rank` is the order: changed 0, removed 1, added 2,
  derived_changed 3, indeterminate 4, derived_indeterminate 5. The kind's
  number in the formats (indeterminate 3, derived_changed 4) does not move;
  the derived `Ord` of `FindingKind` is that of the numbers and orders
  nothing. The verdict reader rejects findings not ascending by
  `(rank, file, key)`.

## Records (§2.3 "Records")

- One record per distinct `(id, name, class, kind, flag, Key)`: two units
  that give the same record (two equal content-keyed units, two namespaces
  merged by a rule) are one. Sorted by `(id, name)`, then class, kind,
  flag, `Key`.
- The order unit's `Key` is `{order, root, order, none}` under the rules.
- A unit whose entity has no key (`KeyRes::None`) has `key: None` (a
  fallback key). A unit whose `Key`, computed again, is none or has
  another id is an `Err` naming it; no record is guessed.
- A unit's name must be among the walk's `Output::names`; else `Err`.

## Declared changes by name (§2.3)

- Lines end at `\n`; a `\r` before it is part of the end of line (CRLF is
  accepted). A line blank, or whose first non-blank is `#`, is ignored.
  Blanks are space and tab. The keyword is followed by a blank.
- `relocate`: split at the first ` => `; the right side at its last `::`.
  The name is an identifier (`[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*`) or `=`. An empty
  parent is the global namespace.
- `[kind]` is a kind's name as `KeyKind::name` gives it; another, or a
  missing `]`, or nothing after it, is `Syntax`.
- `NoUnit` lists at most eight names of the image that contain the last
  component of the text (after the last `::` outside parentheses, cut at
  `(` or `<`), in name order, then `…` if there are more.
- `Ambiguous`: the units of that name (and kind) have more than one
  `KeyId`. Two records of one id are not ambiguous. Any record of that id
  without a `Key` makes it `Fallback`.
- `m(P')`: the chain of parents of `P'` is followed through the post
  records (at most 4096 levels) up to the first parent that is not a unit
  whose `Key` the records hold; from there down each `Key` is given its
  mapped parent and mapped by the rules of the lines above. A parent whose
  record is missing is kept unmapped, with everything above it: a rule
  built on it does not match, which verification reports.
- `Duplicate`: a `relocate` whose `(parent, kind, name, disc)` equals an
  earlier one's; a key twice in one exemption list. Reported at the later
  line. One entity relocated by two lines to two places is two rules.
- A rule the rule table refuses (a name no reader accepts) is
  `NotRenamable` at its line; no walk gives such a key.
- `resolve` reads `relocate`, `changed`, `removed` in file order, `finish`
  reads `created`; each returns the first error in file order.

## The run's report (§7)

- `state` is `failed` for: no shard; a shard of a phase other than
  verify; a shard `validate` rejects; status failed; an ok shard without a
  verdict; a verdict whose six by-kind counters are missing, do not sum to
  its total, disagree with `verify.findings`, or count fewer of a kind than
  it lists. Diagnosed or rejected is `not_parsed` if the map shard is ok,
  else `never_parsed`. `unchanged` is never produced by `state`.
- `Report::add` counts as failed a shard of another observer or round
  than the one it is added as.
- Used exemptions are counted from verified and mismatch states only;
  findings from mismatch only. `findings.total` saturates.
- A finding group is keyed by `(kind, key)`; a group of no key renders
  `key` as null in JSON and `-` in text. The name shown is the first
  non-empty one in observer order.
- Text: at most 100 groups; a path's bytes that are not printable ASCII
  as `\xNN`; a key as its first 16 hex digits; trailing empty words
  omitted.
- `Report::error_lines` (beyond the interface of §7.3): the stderr lines,
  one per observer not verified, at most 100, then
  `slip: and N more (see the report)`.
