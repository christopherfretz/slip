# WP-022 — slip_model: keys, names, rules and the `DECL` container

What §2 leaves open, as `slip_model::declared`, `slip_model::keys` and
`slip_verify::Exemptions` now implement it. Encodings, the container and
its JSON form are `spec/landed/model/WP-022.md`.

- **Rule order.** "Sorted by (parent, kind, name, disc)" is the order of the
  bytes of the encoding of those four fields, in that order (a rule's
  match); `Rule`'s derived `Ord` is not that order and is not used for it.
  Byte order puts a shorter identifier first (`b` before `aa`), because
  `bytes` begins with its length.
- **`rules_id`** is `RuleTable::id()`: `H("slip.rules.v1", list<Rule>)` over
  the rules in that order, each with all six fields.
- **One step.** A rule matches a key, already mapped bottom-up, when its
  match equals the key's `(parent, kind, name, disc)`; the key's parent and
  name become `to_parent` and `to_name`; kind and discriminator are kept.
  Rules do not chain: a swap of `f` and `g` is two rules and maps each name
  once.
- **Exemptions from declared changes.** `Exemptions::from_declared(&Declared)`
  is the three lists of the declared changes, unchanged; the rules are not
  exemptions (the walk applies them, §2.1).
- **`Declared::none()`** is four empty lists; its `rules().id()` is the empty
  table's id, `H("slip.rules.v1", 00)`.

Tests: `crates/slip_model/tests/keys.rs` (rule tables),
`crates/slip_model/tests/declared.rs`, and
`crates/slip_verify/tests/compare.rs` `s2_exemptions_from_declared`.
