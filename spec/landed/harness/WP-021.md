# WP-021 — slip_kiln: a snippet front end for tests, and who may name Kiln's types

`kiln-boundary` (`spec/harness.md` §3) gains a third rule, after the two of
the table: **outside `crates/slip_kiln/`, the paths `slip_kiln::kiln` and
`slip_kiln::snippet` are named only in files under `crates/slip_digest/`
and `crates/slip_script/`; `slip_kiln::snippet` also in files under
`crates/<c>/tests/` for each crate `<c>` that may link Kiln (`slip_kiln`,
`slip_digest`, `slip_script`, `slip_cli`).**

## Rules

- The files checked are the listed files whose path ends in `.rs`, other
  than those under `crates/slip_kiln/`.
- The check is textual, over tokens: line comments, nested block comments,
  string literals in every form (`"…"`, `b"…"`, `c"…"`, raw strings with any
  number of `#`), character and byte literals are not code. A `'` that does
  not open a character literal (a lifetime or a label) is not one.
- A name is the token `slip_kiln`, the token `::` (spaces, line breaks and
  comments may separate tokens), then either
  - the token `kiln` or `snippet` (the path form; a leading `::` before
    `slip_kiln` changes nothing), or
  - a `{`: every item of that group, at the group's own brace depth, whose
    first token (after the `{` or a `,`) is `kiln` or `snippet` (the `use`
    group form; `slip_kiln::{x::{kiln}}` names `x::kiln`, not a module of
    `slip_kiln`).
- A violation is at the line of the `kiln` or `snippet` token. Messages:
  - ``names `slip_kiln::kiln`; only crates/slip_digest/ and
    crates/slip_script/ may``
  - ``names `slip_kiln::snippet`; only crates/slip_digest/,
    crates/slip_script/ and the tests/ of slip_kiln, slip_digest,
    slip_script and slip_cli may``
- Not caught, by design of a textual check: a rename of the crate
  (`use slip_kiln as k; k::kiln`) and a glob (`use slip_kiln::*`).
