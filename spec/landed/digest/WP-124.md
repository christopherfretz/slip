# WP-124 — what a pattern keeps outside the AST: a template constructor's mem-initializers, and the audit of its siblings

What `crates/slip_digest` does for a constructor definition whose
mem-initializers Kiln keeps off the AST (`spec/digest.md` §10,
`spec/digest-kinds.md` `kiln_ast::decl::CtorInit`). Kiln references are
at the pinned Kiln (`kiln/` in the checkout). Pair directories are under
`crates/slip_digest/tests/`.

## Item 2: the rule

`decl_class.rs` `ctor_inits` writes `ColdDeclData` `ctor_inits` for both
callers, `decl_scope::cold` (a constructor's own unit) and
`decl_func::function_definition` (its resolved definition). It is
indeterminate `Unsupported("kiln/pattern-mem-initializers")` when
`mem_inits_outside_ast` holds: `d` is a `CXXConstructor` with a body,
and it is `INVALID`, or it is `DEPENDENT_CTX`, or its class is
`DEPENDENT_CTX`.

Kiln's condition (`kiln_sema/src/class/ctorinit.rs`
`act_on_mem_initializers`, `check_mem_inits_of`): the list is checked
and stored in `ctor_inits` when the constructor is not `INVALID`, its
class is not `DEPENDENT_CTX`, and `!in_template()`
(`templ/mod.rs` `in_template`: `dependent_depth > 0`); otherwise it goes
to `InstState::pattern_mem_inits`, when not empty, for instantiation to
substitute (`templ/class.rs` `function_def`). The parser calls
`act_on_mem_initializers` for every constructor definition
(`kiln_parse/src/class.rs`). `in_template()` is read from the AST
through the definition's own `DEPENDENT_CTX`: Kiln's builder sets it on
every declaration made while `dependent_depth > 0`
(`kiln_ast/src/builder.rs` `add`, `TemplateTables::dependent_head`), and
the definition's declaration is made by its declarator, inside every
head its body is in. That is what catches a constructor template of a
plain class, an abbreviated one, a local class in a function template or
generic lambda, and a definition outside its class. An instantiation
saves and zeroes `dependent_depth` (`templ/class.rs` instantiation), so
an instantiated constructor is not `DEPENDENT_CTX` and keeps its list;
an explicit specialisation is not under an open head.

No narrower test exists with the AST alone. The AST has no mark of
whether a ctor-initializer was written: `pattern_mem_inits` keeps no
entry for an empty list, and nothing on the declaration says either.
`FunctionData::declarator_end` is recorded only for declarations
without a body; `init_fp` only under a floating-point pragma; the
declaration-extent and written-reference tables are opt-in
(`-fkiln-decl-extents`), which the digest cannot require. The list's
expressions are in the arena, linked from nothing; an empty `m()` has
no expression at all. The only other evidence is the source text
between `)` and `{`, which the digest does not read. So every
constructor definition of the condition is indeterminate, with a
ctor-initializer or without (`indeterminate/_/mem-initializer-none-in-template`):
the Tests item "a constructor in a template without a ctor-initializer:
as determinate as it was" cannot hold until Kiln's record
distinguishes the two (For Kiln 1).

## Item 5: when Kiln's record lands (WP-3026)

- `decl_class.rs` `mem_inits_outside_ast` and `PATTERN_MEM_INITS` go;
  `ctor_inits` reads the record's list for a pattern constructor as it
  reads `ctor_inits` today (the expressions are pattern expressions:
  `expr.rs` already writes dependent ones). If the record exists only
  for a non-empty list, a pattern constructor without a ctor-initializer
  needs the record's "none" or the rule stays for it.
- The three pending `differ/_/mem-initializer-*-template` pairs lose
  `# pending` and become `differ`; their `indeterminate` copies, and
  every `indeterminate/_/mem-initializer-*` directory, move to `differ`
  with the lines item 1 lists as "before" plus the pattern's `changed`.
- `resolved/definition-differs/ctor-inits` loses `# pending`.
- The four `meta.h` lines of `indeterminate/Reflectee/*` and
  `indeterminate/_/local-class-reflected` go, as the `meta.h` header
  comment says.
- `spec/digest.md` §10's bullet and `spec/digest-kinds.md`'s
  `CtorInit` sentences go; item 3's 893 units are determinate again.
