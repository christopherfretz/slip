# WP-087 — the keys a transform's declarations need

What `crates/slip_digest/src/query.rs` (`slip_digest::query::Keys`) reads
into `spec/api.md` §7.5, `spec/verify.md` §2.1, §2.3, §9.5 and
`spec/edits.md` §9.1, §9.2, §9.7 where the specs are silent, and the
stricter reading taken each time. The tests are
`crates/slip_digest/tests/api_keys.rs`, named after each decision.

## The interface, and its one departure

As `docs/wp/WP-087.md` gives it, plus a fourth refusal:
`NoKey::NotRenamable`, given only by `rename_rule`. The brief allowed a
`NoKey` or an error of its own; a variant keeps one error type for the
callers (WP-095, WP-071) and cannot be confused with `NotAUnit` (the
entity has a key; it is the rename that is refused). Also public, hidden
from the documentation: `query::walk_units`, the walk's own
`(entity, key)` list, the oracle of test 1.

## Every answer is the walk's

`Keys` holds a `Walk` of its own and calls only the functions the walk
calls: `Walk::entity`, `Walk::key`, `Walk::key_bytes`,
`units::unit_class`, `units::write`, `demand::resolved_units`,
`local::unit_of`, `local::owner`, `local::param_template(s)`. No key
field is derived a second way. The walk does not call `query.rs`, so no
digest depends on it. Outside `query.rs` the change is one `pub mod`
line in `lib.rs` and `pub(crate)` on `local::param_template` and
`local::param_templates`.

`new` and `with_rules` run nothing: no unit is collected and no owner is
numbered ahead of need. A local type's number is computed when first
asked for, and the memo is never sealed. Test 1 checks that this agrees
with the full walk (which numbers every owner first) on 25,326 units of
4,958 pair-test sides. Test `answers_do_not_depend_on_order` checks that
the answers do not depend on the order they are asked in.

## `key_id` and `key`

- **A key the walk computes with a cause** (`KeyRes::Key { cause:
  Some(_) }`: a discriminator or parent with a pending or unsupported
  area, such as a closure in a signature) is `Indeterminate(cause)`.
  It is not given: a key with a cause may not tell the entity apart
  from another (`a_key_with_a_cause_is_indeterminate`).
- **A local entity that is no local type** (`Walk::key` gives
  `key/local`) is `NotAUnit`, as a parameter is.
- **Keys of entities that are not units:** a local type, a member of a
  local class, or a specialisation that is only named have a key that
  some stream writes, so they get `Ok`. `unit_of` tells the caller
  whether the entity is a unit.
- **Collisions and taints are not seen.** A key that collides in the
  translation unit (digest §3.4) is still given. So is the key of a
  unit that a declaration without a key kind taints. The walk makes
  both units indeterminate, so whatever is declared about them
  compares unequal anyway (`spec/verify.md` §9.5).
- `key` reads `key_id` first, so the two refuse alike by construction.
  Then `key` rebuilds the key's bytes (`Walk::key_bytes`, the function
  the records read) and checks the id. A mismatch would be an internal
  defect and is `Indeterminate(query/key-again)`; it never occurs
  (test 8).

## `unit_of`

- **The innermost unit**, following `Walk::entity` and the semantic
  context. A default member initialiser is held by its field, since the
  field's stream holds it (digest-kinds §5.5 `FieldData`); digest §2.1
  allows the class or the field.
- **A local entity's unit** is `local::unit_of`'s: its owner, or the
  owner's unit when the owner is written in place. A closure in a
  namespace variable's initialiser is held by the variable, though its
  context is the translation unit.
- **A closure's static invoker** (`__invoke`) is implicit and has a
  body, so it is a derived unit even though its class is local
  (`local::derived_under_local`). It holds its own copy of the call
  operator's parameters.
- **A template parameter** is held by its template's unit
  (`local::param_template`; Kiln gives it the template's context).
- **No unit** (`None`): the translation unit, a lifetime-extended
  temporary, and a declaration whose chain of contexts reaches no unit.

## `derived`

The brief's "its resolved properties, the instantiations of it and of
its members" is read as follows:

- The resolved and `default_argument` units of `e` itself, when it is a
  unit.
- **Instantiations:** every entity reached from `e` by edges that
  include at least one instantiation edge. Instantiation edges are:
  - a specialisation from its template, and from the pattern it was
    instantiated from;
  - a member of a specialisation from the member it was instantiated
    from;
  - anything with Kiln's `instantiated_from`, from that.

  The other edges are a member from its class (semantic and lexical
  context) and a local type from its owner. This reaches members at any
  depth of classes and the instantiations of local types owned by `e`.
  No edge leaves a namespace for its members.
- Of those, only units of class `Derived` are listed, with their own
  resolved and `default_argument` units. A derived unit that is
  written only on demand (an implicit member nothing defines) is left
  out, since whether it exists depends on other units.
- **A class's implicit members are not derived from the class.** The
  walk makes them derived units, but they are not instantiations
  (`derived_lists_instantiations_and_resolved_units`, `I`).

## `rename_rule`

- The rule is read from `key(e)`. Both parents are the key's parent
  (the same scope), `c_linkage` on both sides for an entity with C
  language linkage (verify §2.3, `c_linkage_rule_keeps_its_parent`).
  The kind and discriminator are the key's. `name` is
  `identifier(new_name)` and `to_name` is the key's name. On a
  `with_rules` `Keys` the parent is in mapped terms, which is what a
  table that already renames the enclosing scope needs.
- **`NotRenamable`:**
  - a key of kind `specialisation`, `partial_specialisation` or
    `decomposition`, whose name only follows its template or its first
    binding (the rules WP-103 forbids a table to contain);
  - a key whose name is no identifier (an operator, a constructor, an
    unnamed class, the unnamed namespace);
  - a `new_name` that is not `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*` or that equals
    the current name.

  An unnamed class named by a renamed variable is refused. Its follower
  rule is built from `key`, as `spec/edits.md` §9.2 says, and test
  `rule_of_an_unnamed_class_from_its_key` checks that this works.

## What waits for WP-103

On today's main, three renames do not give equal digests (test 4). Tests
7 and 8 already hold for them. The ignored tests are:

- `pending_rename_class_template_with_instantiation`: a
  specialisation's key takes its template's unmapped name.
- `pending_rename_member_of_class_template_with_instantiation`: the
  rule on `S::m` does not reach `S<int>::m`.
- `pending_rename_structured_binding`: the decomposition's key keeps
  its first binding's unmapped name.

## Mutations

Each mutation is a single change to `query.rs`. All but one are killed:

| Mutation | Killed by |
|---|---|
| `entity` gives `d` (wrong entity for a redeclaration) | `keys_agree_with_the_walk_on_every_pair_test` |
| `entity` gives `first(d)` (a pattern is its own entity) | `keys_agree_with_the_walk_on_every_pair_test` |
| `unit_of` one level early (any keyed entity is a unit) | `unit_of_is_the_innermost_unit` |
| `unit_of` one level late (starts from the context) | `unit_of_is_the_innermost_unit`, `each_refusal` |
| `unit_of` ignores `local::unit_of` | `unit_of_is_the_innermost_unit` |
| `derived` without `e`'s resolved units | `derived_lists_instantiations_and_resolved_units` |
| `derived` without member instantiations | `derived_lists_instantiations_and_resolved_units` |
| `derived` without the instantiations' resolved units | `derived_lists_instantiations_and_resolved_units` |
| `derived` follows non-instantiation edges as instantiations | `derived_lists_instantiations_and_resolved_units` |
| `rename_rule` with a wrong parent on one side | `rule_fields`, `c_linkage_rule_keeps_its_parent`, `rename_rule_undoes_a_rename` |
| `rename_rule` with the names swapped | `rule_fields`, `c_linkage_rule_keeps_its_parent`, `rule_of_an_unnamed_class_from_its_key`, `rename_rule_undoes_a_rename` |
| `rename_rule` drops the discriminator | `rule_fields`, `rename_rule_undoes_a_rename` |
| `rename_rule` allows (partial) specialisations | `rename_rule_refusals` |
| `with_rules` ignores the table | `rename_rule_undoes_a_rename` |
| `key` refuses unlike `key_id` | `each_refusal`, `keys_agree_with_the_walk_on_every_pair_test` |
| `key_id` gives `Ok` for a key with a cause | `a_key_with_a_cause_is_indeterminate` |
| `key_id` makes a local entity indeterminate | `each_refusal` |
| `key_id` not taken to the entity first | equivalent: `Walk::key` takes it there itself, and the content check reads a kind that every redeclaration shares |

"The mapped parent on the wrong side" has no direct form here, because
both parents are the key's parent. It is modelled as a wrong parent on
one side.
